# Stock capture protocol - draft for field validation

Route 2. Install **Stray Scanner** from its [official App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/stray-scanner/id1557051662) for LiDAR. Use Apple's Camera app for photos or video. Record the app version, iPhone model and iOS version. The protocol has not been tested in person and the photo/video implementation is not ready for the case study's accuracy gates.

**Prepare.** Turn on room lights. Keep people and pets out of the route. Open interior doors. Do not move furniture during recording. Identify rooms in walking order, including corridors. Preserve mirrors, glass and shiny or wet surfaces in the real scene; record their locations separately. Do not stage dangerous damage. Use removable markings for simulated stains/cracks and keep their labels separate from pipeline inputs.

**LiDAR, a Pro-class iPhone with LiDAR.** Open Stray Scanner and start a recording. Hold the phone steadily near chest height in landscape orientation. In each room slowly sweep the entire floor, all wall edges, door/window jambs and ceiling; spend 30-60 seconds per room. Walk the perimeter with at least half-view overlap, approximately 0.3 metres per second, staying 0.5-3 metres from surfaces. Pause at openings to view both jambs and the top from two angles. Pass through every connector; include the return path to the original starting location. End recording only after completing the loop. Review that floor and ceiling were actually seen. Export the original recording folder/ZIP, including `rgb.mp4`, `depth/`, `confidence/`, `odometry.csv`, `camera_matrix.csv` and `imu.csv`. Never resize depth PNGs or re-encode the video. Transfer by Files/AirDrop/USB as the app allows. The exported files must match the documented Stray Scanner format; app UI/export actions need field verification.

**Photos, any iPhone 15 or newer.** Use Camera Photo mode, main 1x camera, no digital zoom or panorama. Take 2-8 landscape photos per room from separated corners, moving approximately 0.5 metres between overlapping views rather than rotating in place. Include wall/floor and wall/ceiling junctions. Take at least two views of each connector, one from each side. Save original images as JPEG, with one folder per room and a connector folder; convert HEIC to JPEG if required. Keep a separate room/door mapping note for later independent assessment. This note is not an automatic stitching result.

**Video, any iPhone 15 or newer.** Use Camera Video mode at 1080p/30 fps, main 1x camera, landscape orientation. Follow the LiDAR walking route, spending 30-60 seconds per room. Avoid fast pans and exposure changes. Save the original MP4; convert an incompatible container to MP4 while preserving frames if necessary.

**Avoid.** Darkness, motion blur, covering the camera/LiDAR, excessive close-ups, changing lenses, walking through mirrors or aiming only at furniture. Include difficult surfaces deliberately, with a note about their location. Invisible concealed damage cannot be diagnosed from these captures.

**Handoff.** Give the operator the raw ZIP, video or photo-folder tree. Preserve raw files. Run one command: `python -m roomscan run INPUT --out output/CAPTURE`. Review `review.html`. Missing ceiling or unobservable scale is reported as unresolved; it does not authorize guessing measurements.
